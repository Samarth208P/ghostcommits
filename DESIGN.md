# Interface direction

Developer workspace with flat near-black surfaces, bold white headings, restrained red brand accents, precise borders, and generous spacing. Primary actions use a solid white fill. Inter is self-hosted and used throughout; monospace is reserved for code and identifiers. Ghost branding appears in the logo and identity avatar.

The desktop shell has a compact sidebar, quiet header, broad working area, and contextual right rail. Mobile navigation wraps into a compact top row, with network and identity access retained. Bounty details, reviewable submissions, evidence-based timelines, identity recovery, and sponsor actions are the core surfaces. Supplemental explanations live behind info controls that support hover, keyboard focus, and tap. Privacy documentation, important warnings, acceptance criteria, and payment terms remain directly readable.

The background is static: no stars, mesh, glow, orbit, pulsing accents, or animated borders. Controls use short 125–200ms feedback transitions. Popovers, dialogs, and toasts ease in and out; reduced motion removes spatial movement while retaining gentle visual feedback. Only loading indicators loop while work is pending.

The sidebar retracts with the header toggle on desktop and mobile. Its preference persists locally across navigation and reloads. Retracting makes the sidebar inert, expands the workspace, and keeps the name visible in the header. Toggling preserves live form fields and keyboard focus. Sidebar and workspace movement use a short transform transition, with immediate positioning for reduced motion.

Green means verified; amber means pending; neutral states must not imply success. No invented contributors, reputation, transactions, or activity. An unconfigured bounty is explicitly a sample. Keyboard focus, reduced motion, loading, errors, and empty states are first-class.
