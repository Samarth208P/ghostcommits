# Interface direction

Developer workspace with charcoal surfaces, muted violet primary controls, warm white text, precise borders, and generous spacing. Inter is self-hosted; monospace is reserved for code and identifiers. Ghost branding appears in the logo and identity avatar.

The desktop shell has a compact sidebar, quiet header, broad working area, and contextual right rail. Mobile navigation wraps into a compact top row. Bounty details, reviewable submissions, evidence-based timelines, identity recovery, and sponsor actions are the core surfaces.

Green means verified; amber means pending; neutral states must not imply success. No invented contributors, reputation, transactions, or activity. An unconfigured bounty is explicitly a sample. Keyboard focus, reduced motion, loading, errors, and empty states are first-class.
